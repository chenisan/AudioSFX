# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## 我們的承諾 · Our Pledge

為了營造開放友善的環境，我們承諾讓參與本專案與社群的每個人都不受騷擾，無論年齡、體型、身心障礙、族裔、性別認同與表達、經驗、國籍、外貌、種族、宗教或性取向。

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 標準 · Our Standards

有助於正向環境的行為 / Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- 展現同理與善意 · Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others
- 尊重不同意見與經驗 · Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- 給予並樂於接受建設性回饋 · Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- 為錯誤負責並向受影響者致歉 · Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes

不可接受的行為 / Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- 性化的言語或意象、性騷擾 · Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- 嘲諷、侮辱或人身攻擊 · Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- 公開或私下騷擾 · Public or private harassment
- 未經許可公開他人私人資訊 · Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## 執行 · Enforcement

如遇騷擾或不當行為，請聯絡專案維護者：**isan@poofone.com.tw**。
所有檢舉都會被審視並適當處理，且尊重檢舉者的隱私與安全。

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at **isan@poofone.com.tw**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with respect for the privacy and security of the reporter.
